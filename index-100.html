Overview
01

Welcome to 8Bird — a fun, educational number-matching puzzle game designed for players of all ages. This Privacy Policy explains clearly and honestly how we collect, use, and protect your information when you play.

By playing 8Bird, you agree to the practices described here. If you have any concerns, please reach out to us — we're real people and we genuinely care.

✅
8Bird was built with a privacy-first mindset. Your game data stays on your device. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.
GDPR Compliant COPPA Safe CCPA Ready PDPA Aligned
🗂️
What We Collect
02

We collect only what is absolutely necessary to deliver a smooth gaming experience. Nothing more.

💾
Game Progress: Level completions, star ratings, high scores, and coins — stored entirely in your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device.
⚙️
Preferences: Sound, music, vibration, and language settings — saved locally so your experience is consistent across sessions.
📱
Device Info: Basic technical data like device type, browser version, and screen size may be collected automatically to ensure the game displays correctly on your screen.
📊
Anonymous Analytics: Gameplay patterns (such as which levels are most played) may be collected in anonymized form to help us improve the game. This data cannot identify you personally.
ℹ️
We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily contact us.
⚡
How We Use Your Information
03

Every piece of information we collect serves a specific, limited purpose tied directly to your experience:

🎮
Save your progress so you can continue exactly where you left off, every time.
🎵
Remember your settings so you don't have to re-configure the game after every session.
🐛
Fix bugs and improve performance using anonymous crash reports and error logs.
🎯
Design better levels by understanding which parts of the game players enjoy most.
📢
Show relevant advertising (where applicable) to support free access to the game without charging players.
📢
Advertising
04

8Bird may display advertisements from third-party networks such as Google AdMob, which help us keep the game free for everyone.

🔍
Google AdMob may use your device's advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android) to show personalized ads based on your interests.
🚫
Opt Out Anytime: You can disable personalized ads in your device settings. iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking. Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.
🔗
Third-party advertisers have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy
🧒
For users identified as children (under 13), we serve only non-personalized, child-appropriate ads in full compliance with Google Families Policy and COPPA regulations.
🧸
Children's Privacy
05

8Bird is designed to be a safe, enriching experience for players of all ages, including young children. We take this responsibility seriously.

🛡️
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country).
💬
The game contains no chat, social features, or user-generated content — children cannot be contacted by strangers through our game.
🎯
All advertisements shown in child contexts are age-appropriate and non-personalized, complying with Google Families and COPPA.
👨‍👩‍👧
If a parent or guardian believes their child has accidentally provided personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it promptly.
✨
8Bird requires no account creation and no personal information whatsoever to play. Just open and enjoy!
🛡️
Data Storage & Security
06

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

📱
Local Only: Game progress and settings live in your device's localStorage. They are never transmitted to our servers. Clearing your browser or app data will reset your progress.
🔐
Encrypted Transit: Any data that is transmitted (e.g., anonymous analytics) uses industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption.
⏳
Minimal Retention: Any anonymous analytics data we collect is retained for no longer than 12 months, after which it is permanently and irreversibly deleted.
🏢
No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party beyond what is strictly necessary for game functionality and ad delivery.
⚖️
Your Rights
07

Depending on where you live, you may have specific rights regarding your personal data. We respect and honour all of them.

👁️
Right to Access: Request a full copy of any personal data we hold about you.
🗑️
Right to Deletion: Request erasure of your data. For locally stored game data, you can clear it yourself via your browser or app settings instantly.
✏️
Right to Correction: If any information we hold is inaccurate, you can request it be corrected at any time.
🚫
Right to Opt-Out: Opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device's ad settings without any impact on your ability to play the game.
📦
Right to Portability: Request your data in a commonly used, machine-readable format.
📧
To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@8bird.game. We will respond within 30 business days.
